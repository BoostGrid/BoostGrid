Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23 2026

BoostGrid is built and operated by Cenova Global (see About Cenova Global). This page explains what information BoostGrid collects, why, and what you can do about it.

This is a plain-language description of what the app actually does, written directly from its code and data flows — not a generic template. It isn't legal advice, and as BoostGrid grows, we recommend having a lawyer review and formalize it further.

1. Information we collect

Account information (collected at signup):

  • Name, email address, and password (your password is never stored in plain text — it's handled by our authentication provider, Appwrite, using industry-standard hashing)
  • A nickname you choose, and a short bio if you sign up as an admin
  • Your account role — client, earner, or admin at signup; "owner" is an internal role for the platform operator, not something you can sign up as

Financial information (only if you deposit or withdraw funds):

  • Your wallet balance on the platform
  • Bank account name, account number, and bank name, submitted by you when requesting a withdrawal — used solely to send your payout
  • Deposit/withdrawal amounts and transaction references

We do not collect or store your card number, card PIN, or online banking password. Deposits and payouts are processed by Paystack and Flutterwave, who handle that information directly under their own security and compliance programs. We only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded (and the amount), which we verify against their records before crediting your wallet.

Task and project information (if you create or complete tasks):

  • Project/task titles, descriptions, links, and reward amounts you enter
  • When completing a task, the social media platform and profile/handle you submit for verification, and any file/screenshot you upload as proof

Referral information: your referral code, who referred you (if anyone), and how many people you've referred.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create and secure your account, and show you the right dashboard for your role
  • To process deposits and withdrawals, and prevent fraudulent transactions
  • To connect clients with earners and track task/project progress
  • To calculate and pay referral rewards
  • To moderate the platform — for example, tasks can be flagged, and accounts with excessive flags are restricted, to keep the platform fair for everyone

3. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We share the minimum necessary with:

  • Appwrite — hosts our authentication and database (where your account and platform data live)
  • Paystack and Flutterwave — process deposits and withdrawals
  • Other users, where the platform is designed to show it — e.g. a client sees the name of the admin managing their project, and vice versa; task proof/screenshots you submit are visible to the admin reviewing that task

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is active. Financial records (deposits, withdrawals, transaction references) are kept longer, since we may need them for fraud investigation, dispute resolution, or if required by payment processors or, eventually, financial regulation.

5. Your choices

  • You can update your profile info (name, nickname, bio, profile picture) at any time from your dashboard
  • You can request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account, by emailing us (below) — note that we may need to retain some financial records even after a deletion request, for the reasons in section 4

6. Security

All pages are served over HTTPS. Authentication and password handling are managed by Appwrite. Sensitive financial actions (verifying deposits, paying withdrawals, moving wallet balances) run through server-side functions rather than directly from your browser, so they can't be tampered with by manipulating the app.

7. Children

BoostGrid is not intended for anyone under 18. Signing up requires a bank account for withdrawals and involves real money, so it isn't designed or suitable for children, regardless of local age of majority. If we learn that an account belongs to a child, we will take steps to remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page and let you know through the app.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email boostgrid1@gmail.com.
