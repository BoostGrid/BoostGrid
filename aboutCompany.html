About Cenova

BoostGrid is a product built and operated by Cenova.

Cenova is the name under which we build and run our digital products — BoostGrid is the second one of them. Think of Cenova Global as the team or brand behind the platform, and BoostGrid as the web you're using today.

One company, several products

Cenova is a Global company. Rather than running every individual product as its own separate entity, we operate our apps — BoostGrid included — under this one company, the way many small software businesses run several products under a single parent entity.

How we protect your money and data

Deposits and withdrawals on BoostGrid go through licensed payment processors (Paystack and Flutterwave) — we never handle your card or bank login details directly, and every deposit is verified against the processor's own records before your balance is credited. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what data we collect and why, and our Platform Rules for how the platform itself is expected to be used.
