Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using BoostGrid

General

What is BoostGrid?

BoostGrid connects clients, who want engagement on their social media content, with earners, who complete tasks (like liking, following, or quote-tweeting a post) and get paid for it. Admins review submitted work and release payment.

What account types are there?

At signup you can choose Client or Earner. There's also an Admin role for people reviewing tasks and managing payouts, and an internal Owner role for the platform operator — owner accounts aren't self-service and can't be selected during signup.

Which social platforms does BoostGrid support?

Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, Twitter/X, Snapchat, Threads, LinkedIn, YouTube, and Telegram are all supported as named platforms. A task can also be marked "No platform required" for work that isn't tied to a specific social app, or as a fully custom task with its own instructions.

For Earners

How do I get my first task?

When you sign up, BoostGrid automatically looks for any task that's currently unclaimed (up to one per project) and assigns it to you right away. If nothing's available at that exact moment, new tasks will still reach you as clients keep creating projects.

Can I get more than one task from the same project?

No — you'll never be assigned two tasks from the same project. Each project only ever gives one task per earner, whether it's assigned when the project is created or picked up automatically after you sign up.

What do I need to submit a task?

It depends on the task. For a named platform (like Instagram), you'll need to have your handle saved in your profile settings — the task will show you what to do and use your saved handle automatically. For a custom task, you'll type in whatever's requested. Every task also requires you to upload proof (usually a screenshot) before submitting.

Do I need to fill out my profile before I can work?

Yes — your Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp details are required as a baseline before you can submit any task, even if a particular task doesn't use those platforms. Other platforms (Twitter/X, TikTok, etc.) only need to be filled in if a task specifically requires them.

How and when do I get paid?

Once you submit proof for a task, an admin reviews it. If approved, the reward is added to your wallet balance immediately. There's currently no fixed deadline for how long review takes.

What happens if my submission gets flagged?

A client can flag a submission that doesn't meet requirements. This doesn't count against you right away — it goes to an admin for review first. If the admin confirms the flag, it adds to a running total on your account, and reaching a high enough total (equivalent to 3 confirmed flags) will automatically ban your account. There's currently no in-app appeal step for a confirmed flag, so if you believe one was a mistake, contact support directly.

For Clients

How does creating a project work?

You set a task description, a reward amount per earner, and how many earners you want engaging with it. BoostGrid then either assigns the task directly to existing earners (one each, up to your engagement number) or holds the remainder for new earners as they sign up — until every requested engagement is filled.

What does it cost to run a project?

You pay the reward amount for every earner you're requesting, plus a platform fee of 50% on top of that engagement value. For example, requesting 10 engagements at ₦50 each means ₦500 in rewards, plus ₦250 in platform fees.

Can I get more earners once my project is live?

No — the number of engagements you request at creation is fixed for that project. To get more engagement, create a new project.

What happens if I'm not happy with a submission?

You can flag it. It doesn't count against the earner immediately — an admin reviews every flag first, and only confirms it if it holds up. Repeated or unjustified flagging can lead to restrictions on your own account, so flags should reflect genuine issues with the work.

Payments & Withdrawals

How do I fund my account?

Deposits are processed through Paystack or Flutterwave. BoostGrid never sees or stores your card number or banking password — it only receives confirmation that a payment succeeded, which is verified before your balance is credited.

What's the minimum withdrawal, and are there fees?

The minimum withdrawal is ₦1,000, and each withdrawal has a flat fee of ₦100, deducted from the amount you're withdrawing.

How does the referral program work?

Share your referral code or link. Anyone who joins with your code gets ₦100 added to their balance. Once 10 people have joined with your code, you get a one-time bonus of ₦500.

Account & Safety

Is my password safe?

Yes — your password is never stored in plain text. Authentication is handled by Appwrite using industry-standard hashing, and all sensitive account and payment actions run through secure server-side functions rather than directly from your browser.

Can I switch between light and dark mode?

Yes — most pages have a small toggle button (usually top-right) to switch themes, and your choice is remembered the next time you visit.

Is there a minimum age to use BoostGrid?

BoostGrid is not intended for anyone under 18. It involves real money and bank withdrawals, so it isn't designed or suitable for children.

What happens if my account is banned?

A banned account loses access to its dashboard. If you think this happened in error, reach out to support with your account details — see the contact note below.
