Built for both sides of the task

Whether you're posting a project or completing one, here's what your dashboard looks like.

For Clients

Launch a project in minutes

Set your task, your reward per earner, and how many people you want engaging — BoostGrid handles the rest.

  • Create tasks for any supported platform, or a fully custom request
  • Deposit securely via Paystack or Flutterwave
  • Track projects in progress and completed, all in one place
  • Flag submissions that don't meet your requirements
For Earners

Complete tasks, get paid

Sign up and you're automatically matched with an available task — no hunting required.

  • One task per project, so there's no double-dipping or confusion
  • Upload proof and submit for admin review
  • Approved tasks pay straight into your wallet balance
  • Earn extra through referrals — ₦100 when you join with a code, plus ₦500 once 10 friends join with yours

Admins keep everything running fairly

Every task submission passes through review before a naira changes hands.

What admins actually do

Admins review submitted proof, approve or reject tasks, and release payment from their own balance — keeping clients and earners both protected.

Review submissionsSee exactly what an earner submitted and decide: approve, reject, or flag.
Release paymentApproving a task pays the earner immediately from the admin's balance.
Handle disputesEscalate a submission to the client, or reinstate a task that was wrongly rejected.
Track project healthSee projects in progress and completed at a glance, with a running transaction history.

Why BoostGrid

The details that make the platform work for everyone using it.

🔒

Secure by design

Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text, and sensitive actions like payments run through secure server-side functions.

💳

Trusted payment rails

Deposits and withdrawals go through Paystack and Flutterwave — BoostGrid never sees your card or banking details.

🎯

One task, one earner, one project

You'll never be double-assigned to the same project — every earner gets at most one task per project, guaranteed.

🤝

Referral rewards

Join with a friend's code and get ₦100. Refer 10 friends and earn a ₦500 bonus.

🛡️

Fair moderation

Every submission is reviewed by an admin before payment — and repeated flags are the only thing that can restrict an account.

🌗

Light or dark, your call

Switch themes anywhere in the app — your preference is remembered across every page.
